## AUTHORIZATION DECISION

Pursuant to the authority vested in the Authorizing Official under FISMA (44 U.S.C. § 3554), OMB Circular A-130 (Managing Information as a Strategic Resource), and NIST SP 800-37 Rev 2 (Risk Management Framework for Information Systems), I hereby grant an **AUTHORIZATION TO OPERATE** for the information system identified above.

This authorization is based on my review and acceptance of the residual risk as documented in the Security Assessment Report (SAR) dated [PLACEHOLDER — SAR date], the Plan of Action & Milestones (POA&M), and supporting System Security Plan (SSP) for NHLBI-RDP.

---

## AUTHORIZATION CONDITIONS

This ATO is granted subject to the following conditions:

1. **Continuous Monitoring.** The ISSO shall maintain an active continuous monitoring program as defined in the Continuous Monitoring Strategy and the System Security Plan, Section 6. Monthly vulnerability scans, quarterly control reviews, and annual security assessments shall be conducted and findings reported to the AO.

2. **POA&M Remediation.** All open findings documented in the POA&M shall be remediated on schedule. Any item exceeding its scheduled completion date by more than 30 days must be escalated to the AO in writing within 5 business days.

3. **Significant Change Notification.** The ISSO shall notify the AO of any significant change to the system (as defined in NIST SP 800-128 and the Configuration Management Plan) prior to implementation. Changes categorized as Transformative under the cATO change classification framework require a full security impact analysis and AO re-authorization before deployment.

4. **Incident Reporting.** Security incidents shall be reported per the Incident Response Plan and US-CERT reporting requirements. The AO shall be notified within 1 hour of any confirmed incident affecting Confidentiality, Integrity, or Availability at the Moderate or High level.

5. **Supply Chain Risk.** All third-party software components shall be reviewed for known vulnerabilities (CVEs) prior to deployment. Any component with an unmitigated Critical or High CVE shall not be promoted to production without AO-approved compensating controls.
